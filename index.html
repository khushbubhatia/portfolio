Verity

AI fact-checker. Live. Built Feb 2026.

Paste a claim, get a verdict backed by recent news articles.

Who it is for
Anyone who sees a claim online and wants a quick check before sharing it.
Problem
Checking a claim by hand means opening many tabs and comparing sources.
What I built
A multi-stage AI pipeline. It searches recent news with GNews, reads the articles, and returns a verdict with a short summary. A later stage validates the answer before it is shown.
Decisions I made
  • Verity only checks claims against recent news, not general facts. This keeps each verdict tied to articles you can read.
  • When the browser blocked requests in production (a CORS error), I built a proxy server instead of dropping the news source.
  • When Groq retired the AI model the app used, analysis stopped working. I switched models and the app returned to normal.
Known limits
It cannot check claims that have no recent news coverage. In my testing, one real headline returned "No articles found". I have not confirmed the cause yet.

RentFlag

Rental listing analyzer. Live. Apr to May 2026.

Paste a rental listing and see what to question before you reply or visit.

Who it is for
Renters who read many listings and miss warning signs.
Problem
Listings are written to sell. Renters usually spot problems only after they visit.
What I built
A web app that sends the listing text to an AI model and returns the flags in plain language.
Decisions I made
  • No database and no sign-up. You paste text and get an answer.
  • The AI step works with either Groq or OpenAI, so the app does not depend on one provider.
Known limits
It reads only the text you paste. It cannot check the real property, the landlord, or the photos.

TaskFlow

Real-time Kanban board. Built, not launched. Started Mar 2026.

A task board where changes show up for everyone at once.

Who it is for
Small teams that want a simple board without the weight of a large tool.
What I built
A board with columns and cards, live updates, and a separate frontend and backend, with MongoDB for data.
Decisions I made
  • Drag and drop was hard to use on phones, so on mobile you tap a card to move it.
  • I refined the design over several rounds toward a clean look, close to Linear and Notion.
Known limits
It is not deployed yet, so there is no live link. The code is public.
